Signed documents that cross the air with no network at all.

GhostPacket seals a receipt, a prescription or any message into an Ed25519-signed frame of at most 512 bytes, then sends it from one device to another over sound (a 4-FSK modem) or light (fountain-coded QR codes). The receiver proves it is authentic, or rejects it with an exact reason.

Humanitarian aid vouchers

Distributor · Kaolack flood response

Issues tamper-proof vouchers offline. The anti-replay ledger refuses a voucher presented twice, so it cannot be redeemed twice.

Cryptographic armor

  • Integrity and authenticity: CRC32 catches noise, Ed25519 (strict) catches forgery. Every single-bit flip of every frame is rejected in tests.
  • Optional end-to-end encryption: ChaCha20-Poly1305 with a PBKDF2 passphrase, so a prescription played aloud in a waiting room stays private.
  • Anti-replay ledger and trusted keyring: vouchers cannot be spent twice; unknown issuers are refused.
  • Zero exfiltration: the signing key is born in the tab and never leaves it; no server exists.

Drop it into your app

The GhostPacketSDK wraps the WASM core, Web Audio, the camera, the ledger and the keyring. Seal, send, receive and verify in a few lines, then sync the verified .gpkt proofs to your backend when connectivity returns.

const gp = await GhostPacketSDK.load();
const sealed = gp.sealMessage({ sender, title, message });
if (sealed.ok) await gp.transmitAcoustic(sealed.value.frame);
